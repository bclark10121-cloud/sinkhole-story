## Art made for this game

- Concept pictures, the icon picture and the fisher's portrait (cut to a round picture with `tools/make_portrait.py`): made for this game with Google Gemini image models (Nano Banana Pro) through `tools/nano_banana.py`, from original prompts, on 2026-11-21 (G8.3, G8.8).
- Six 3D models (Snack Shack, giant cone sign, ice cream cart, lifeguard chair, rowboat and beach umbrella): made for this game through Meshy image to 3D from those original pictures, private licence, on 2026-11-21. Shrunk for phones with glTF Transform.

## Sounds made for this game

- All sound effects and the sea and gull ambience are made in code for this game with `tools/make_sfx.py`.
