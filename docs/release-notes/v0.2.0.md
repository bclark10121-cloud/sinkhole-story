# Sinkhole Story v0.2.0

The first real lot: the Snack Shack on Saltbox Pier, in the glossy toy look you picked.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The start screen shows the beach at low tide with the title in big coral letters. A small hole eats a row of shells, then a striped umbrella tips in. Island Meet and Greet plays.
2. Press Play and pick a slot. The fisher tells you how the boardwalk went missing. Close the talk box.
3. Swallow shells and pebbles. Each one plops in. Chee Zee Beach plays. A note says "New in The Haul" the first time you swallow each kind of thing.
4. Grow to size 3 and swallow the boombox. The fisher says the hole learned to thump. A Rumble button shows at the bottom right on touch.
5. Press Rumble near the lifeguard chair. The sand pail on its seat falls off. The music goes muffled for a moment.
6. Open Menu, then The Haul. Each thing you swallowed has a picture and a note from the fisher.
7. Sneak up on a gull slowly and swallow it.
8. Look at the Snack Shack, the cart, the lifeguard chair, the rowboat and the umbrellas up close. These are the six Meshy models.

## New in this version

- The Snack Shack lot: 25 kinds of things, 58 pieces, from seashells to the shack itself.
- Rumble: learned from the boombox. Knocks the sand pail, the sand dollars and the giant cone sign down from their perches.
- The golden shell, hidden under one umbrella.
- Crabs and gulls: swallow them and they pop back out at the end, annoyed and unharmed.
- The Haul: 25 pages, each with a picture and a note from the fisher.
- The finish screen with up to three stars and the percent swallowed.
- Settings: Hint glow and Bigger starting hole. Phone controls now has Follow my finger too.
- All eleven achievements and five stats.
- The fisher's tall tale in the talk box, with his portrait.
- The look: six Meshy models, glossy toy looks for everything else, the crumbly hole, a glossy sea with foam, chunky boardwalk planks.
- The Low tide start screen, the Rammetto One title and Sunny boardwalk colors.
- Music: Island Meet and Greet on the start screen, Chee Zee Beach in play. Soft pops and plops for sounds, plus sea and gull ambience.
- The beach ball icon.
- Short notes no longer cut off at the left edge or cover the finish screen, The Haul or the talk box.
- A piece too big for the hole now stays level over it. Before, the moving hole shoved big pieces into corners.

## Judgment calls

- Look gaps left against your approved picture: the boardwalk is painted flat, the lot reads smaller from the game camera, the sea has no 3D waves, shadows are lighter and the hole's lip is lower and smoother. All listed in the backlog as idea 1.
- The Snack Shack model is drawn 1.3 times bigger than its collision box so the cone sign and the sand dollars line up with it.
- The other two umbrellas are sky blue with a flag and sunflower yellow with a fringe, so they differ by shape too, not by color alone.
- The main button is a deeper coral so white text on it reads clearly.
- Settings, the slot list, Records, Controls and the talk box keep the shared kit colors for now.
- Crabs and gulls are size 2. A size 1 hole cannot catch them. Gulls only notice a hole moving fast.
- Rumble is learned again each lot. The fisher's lines play once per slot.
- The Haul draws its pictures from the game's own models the first time you open it.
- The builder drew the one-color icon used by Android themed icons. Tell me if it reads badly.
