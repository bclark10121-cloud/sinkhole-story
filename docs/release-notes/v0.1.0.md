# Sinkhole Story v0.1.0

The first test build: a gray box of the hole. Plain shapes only. It exists so you can feel how the hole moves, tips things and grows.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game and press Play, then pick a slot. You should be a small dark hole on pale sand within a few seconds.
2. Move under the small coral pieces. Each one should drop in. The ring at the top left fills as you swallow.
3. Fill the ring. The hole should grow with a smooth ease and the camera should pull back a little. The number in the ring goes from 1 to 2.
4. Park under a piece too big for the hole, such as a purple one. It should stay up.
5. Find a tall purple umbrella shape once you reach size 4. It should teeter on the rim, tip and drop.
6. Turn the phone upright and back. The game should keep going and still show what fits next.
7. Open Menu, then Settings, and switch Phone controls to Slide anywhere. Try both.
8. Close the game mid-lot and open it again. Continue should put you back with the same pieces gone.

## New in this version

- The hole: moves, swallows and grows through six sizes.
- One flat test lot with 58 plain pieces. One color per size, so you can read what fits.
- Real physics for the teeter and the fall.
- Touch: a floating stick, with Slide anywhere in Settings. Keyboard and Xbox controller too.
- A ring meter with the size number. A finish note with the percent swallowed.
- Restart the lot in the Menu.
- Four achievements to earn: First Gulp, Growing Up, Clean Beach and Closing Time.
- Tester tools: set the hole size, swallow a whole size, reset the lot and a physics readout.

## Judgment calls

- Real physics held, so no scripted fallback was built.
- The pull on a piece stuck on the rim starts at 1.5 seconds. In forced cases pieces drop at 1.7 to 1.9 seconds. That is close to the 2 second mark and is the first thing to tune after your notes.
- The hole's middle stays inside the lot. A big hole's rim can reach past the edge, so corner pieces stay in reach.
- A big piece over a small hole dips a little and comes back up when the hole leaves.
- Boxes are chunky on purpose. A thin piece would slip into a hole a size too small.
- The start screen is a plain stand-in and the game has no sounds of its own yet. Both come in 0.3.
- No Game Shelf card yet. It comes with 0.3.
