Sinkhole Story

A calm game about a hole with a big appetite

Be the hole. Swallow the boardwalk.

You are a small hole on a seaside boardwalk. Slide under shells and pebbles and they drop in with a plop. Every gulp makes you a little wider, so pails fit, then beach balls, then chairs, umbrellas and a rowboat. Swallow the boombox to learn Rumble and shake things down from high spots. Work your way up to the Snack Shack. No timer, no score and no way to lose.

What kind of game is this?

One thumb. Move the hole. One more button, Rumble, comes later in the lot.
Calm. No timer, no score, no foes and nothing to lose. Leave any time and pick up where you were.
A tall tale. An old fisher tells how a little hole ate the boardwalk. Everything you swallow lands in The Haul with one of the fisher's notes.
Toy box look. A glossy toy beach: striped umbrellas, a lifeguard chair, an ice cream cart and one very unlucky Snack Shack.

Things to do

  • Swallow all 58 things on the Snack Shack lot, from seashells to the shack itself.
  • Grow through six sizes. A ring shows how close the next size is.
  • Learn Rumble from the boombox and thump the pail, the sand dollars and the giant cone sign down from their perches.
  • Fill The Haul: 25 pages, each with a note from the fisher.
  • Earn three stars: swallow the shack, swallow everything and find the golden shell.
  • Sneak up on the crabs and gulls. They pop back out at the end, annoyed and unharmed.
  • Earn eleven achievements.
  • Turn on the hint glow or a bigger starting hole in Settings if you want it easier.

Controls

Keyboard
WASD or arrows move the hole. Space is Rumble. Esc opens the Menu.
Xbox controller
Left stick or D-pad moves the hole. A is Rumble. View or Menu opens the Menu. In menus A chooses and B goes back.
Touch
Put a thumb down and a stick appears under it. Slide to move the hole. The Rumble button sits at the bottom right once you learn it. Settings has Slide anywhere and Follow my finger as other ways to move.

Play with an Xbox controller

Any Xbox Wireless Controller with Bluetooth works. That is every Series X|S controller and the Xbox One controllers made since 2016. If the plastic around the Xbox button matches the face of the controller, it has Bluetooth. Other controllers that your phone or browser sees as a standard gamepad use the same buttons.

On Android
  1. Hold the Xbox button to turn the controller on.
  2. Hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes fast.
  3. On your phone, open Settings, then Connected devices, then Pair new device.
  4. Tap Xbox Wireless Controller. The Xbox button stays lit when it is connected.
  5. Open Sinkhole Story. The touch buttons hide as soon as you use the controller.
In your browser
  1. Connect the controller to your computer with a USB cable or Bluetooth.
  2. Open Play in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  3. Press any button once after the game loads, so the browser sees the controller.
Menus
The D-pad or left stick moves between buttons. A chooses and B goes back. You can play the whole game with only the controller.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on this page.
  2. Open the file. Allow installs from your browser if your phone asks.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
  4. Version 0.2.0 installs over 0.1.0. Your records stay and your slot starts the Snack Shack lot fresh.

Android may show a Play Protect notice for apps from outside the Play Store. Version 0.2.0 installs over the last version and keeps your saves.
